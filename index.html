The Double Helix Map

Most outlines list chapter titles. The Double Helix Map (DHM) gives each chapter a verdict to prove and the advice that goes with it, so the book can’t explain without instructing.

Grand NarrativeThe Story of Thesis. One main sentence per chapter. Read top to bottom, the sentences form a single argument.
DevelopmentThe SAY syntax for the chapter, such as SYA + ASA + YAA, so each module is built in a different order.
Grand AdviceThe Main Advice Statements. For every verdict you deliver, a command or capability the reader can use.

Leave the Advice column empty for a chapter and the book is “unzipped”: a thesis with no instruction. The workspace flags it.

Three chords, one persuasive ARC

A

Awareness

The contextual problem. What has changed, and why the old situation needs attention.

R

Resolution

The methodological problem. The framework or method that answers it.

C

Call to Action

The operational problem. The behaviors and capabilities the reader must adopt.

How many chapters each chord gets depends on your plan: 1-3-1, 2-3-2 or 3-4-2.

SAY: the modules inside each chapter

S: Story

The situated context that hooks the reader.

Y: Yielded Evidence

Just enough proof to hold the story and advice together.

A: Advice

The specific instruction the reader can act on.

A SAY point is a hollow module, about 200–300 words once written. In SAYBOOK you only place it: a one-line micro-sentence and a short note for each part. Syntax strings set the order, for example SYA (story-led), ASA (advice-led) or YAA (evidence-led).

How SAYBOOK works

1. State your wish

Enter your title and your Main Outline Wish: what the book should achieve.

2. Fill the map

Write each chapter’s verdict and advice, choose its SAY syntax, then add subheadings and SAY points.

3. Audit and export

Read the argument as one chain, fix the gaps the audit finds, and print the map as a PDF.

Questions

Is SAYBOOK a writing tool?

No. It plans the book. You build the structure first, then write into it.

Does it write my outline with AI?

No. The map is filled in by you. The audit only checks structure, such as empty fields and repeated syntax. It does not judge whether your argument is good.

Where is my work stored?

In this demo, in your browser only. Accounts and cloud saving come with the full app.

Can I get a PDF?

Yes. The Export tab downloads your map as a PDF. It is a static, print-ready document, not a fillable form; a fillable version is planned.

What about upgrades and the Founding Member limit?

Founding Member places are limited to 50. Upgrade rules will be confirmed before checkout opens.
